Frequently asked questions

What does Thomas Overstreet do?

Thomas Overstreet is a Vehicle Sales Manager at Ryder Vehicle Sales in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, where he sells class 5-8 commercial trucks to fleet and business customers.

What types of trucks does Thomas Overstreet sell?

He specializes in class 5-8 commercial trucks — medium- and heavy-duty vehicles — helping customers spec the right truck for their duty cycle and application.

Where is Thomas Overstreet located?

He is based in Yukon, Oklahoma, and works out of Ryder Vehicle Sales in Oklahoma City.

What is Thomas Overstreet's background before truck sales?

He brings 20+ years in commercial transportation: U.S. Army wheeled vehicle mechanic (91B), customer service representative at Ryder Truck Rental, Regional Parts and Procurement Manager at Paragon Leasing (Stevens Transport), and Maintenance Manager at RWTL Capacity Solutions (Western Flyer Express).

Is Thomas Overstreet a military veteran?

Yes. He served in the U.S. Army as a Light Wheeled Vehicle Mechanic at Fort Hood, Texas, from 2005 to 2007 — including a deployment to Iraq from October 2006 to September 2007 — earning the Iraq Campaign Medal, Global War on Terrorism Service Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Army Service Ribbon, Overseas Service Ribbon, and Driver and Mechanic Badge.
